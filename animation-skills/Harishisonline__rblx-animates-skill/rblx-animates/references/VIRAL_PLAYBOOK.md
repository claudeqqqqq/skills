## 1. What the feed rewards (and what is realistic for us)

- Every Short gets a small cold test (a few hundred viewers who watch Roblox/animation). Subscriber count barely matters
  for that test; the video does. A 25-subscriber channel can break out (itsRedBear: 35.7k subs -> 8.5M in 3 days).
- Gate 1, **stayed to watch** (did not swipe in the first seconds). Ours: IG 3-s skip 18-39 %, YT engaged/views 54-72 %.
  Breakouts are said to clear ~80-85 % **[Gemini, unverified]**.
- Gate 2, **% viewed and rewatches**. Ours: 33-42 % of 96-109 s. Breakouts are said to reach 85-105 % on 35-55 s Shorts,
  75-90 % on 90-150 s **[Gemini, unverified]**. Shorter videos make high % viewed easier.
- Gate 3, **shares and comments**. People send absurd rules, big escalations and twists, not moral lessons. Our moral
  stories (EP3/EP4) got ~0 shares. Comments come from loophole debates, mysteries and "Part 2?" curiosity.
- Honest: even a great Short can stall at 1-10k. Expect many iterations before one clears the gates; one viral post is
  noise, a pattern is a result (growth loop experiments).

## 2. Premise generator

**Formula:** everyday action or trait + an absurd consequence you can SEE + a loophole or underdog flip.

Seven families with original example premises (check each against COMPETITORS.md so we don't repeat a recent hit):

| Family | Examples |
|---|---|
| Body / size rule | every time someone says your name you grow 6 inches, so the popular kid is 40 feet tall and I'm 2 feet; every time you blink your age jumps a year forward or back, and I have a test in 10 minutes |
| Counter over your head | a battery % that only recharges when someone laughs at your joke; 500 footsteps a day, at 0 your legs freeze until midnight; a luck meter stuck at -99 since birth |
| Literal social / truth rule | every broken promise becomes a red brick in your backpack; nobody hears you unless you hold something worth $100; you can only say words starting with your first initial, until the bully steals your name tag |
| World physics glitch | gravity flips for 10 s every time the school bell rings; when my little brother sneezes everyone within 30 feet freezes for 5 minutes; the sun only moves while the mayor is awake, and he just took sleeping pills |
| Power / tier / zero underdog | at 13 you get a power from your worst fear: everyone got fire or flight, I got paper cuts; your pet matches your report card: A's get dragons, my F got a raccoon; everyone has an invisible shield, mine is a neon target for dodgeballs |
| Object becomes real | chalk drawings are real until it rains; whatever goes in your trash comes back 10x bigger at midnight; a sticker gives an object its ability, so I put "rocket" on my skateboard |
| Role swap / reverse economy | students are paid to sleep in class and fined for raising a hand; the loudest person in the room pays everyone's lunch; every Friday kids are the police and adults follow our curfew |

**Score every premise 1-5 on each, produce only at >= 20/25 [Gemini]:**
1. Mute test: the rule reads in 2 s with the sound off.
2. 3x escalation: it naturally gives 3 funnier or higher-stakes examples.
3. Buildable with the kit: scale, floating numbers, props, lighting; no new rigs.
4. Reverse-rule climax: the narrator's curse or zero wins through a loophole.
5. Shareability: would a 14-year-old send it with "bro watch this".
Add from our research (SKILL.md section 2): a personal stake + clock early (family or best friend in danger) and a
visible counter when the premise has a number.

## 3. Script templates (Alex at 3.8-4.0 words/s)

**A. 45-60 s "sprint" (180-225 words)** **[decision: runtime is 90-120 s today]**
| Time | Words | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:03 | 10-14 | rule + visual proof on frame 1 |
| 0:03-0:07 | 15-20 | the cost: why the rule rules daily life |
| 0:07-0:19 | 45-55 | 3 world gags: normal -> weird -> absurd (3-4 s each) |
| 0:19-0:25 | 20-25 | "And me?": the worst / smallest / zero case |
| 0:25-0:31 | 20-25 | "Until one day / night...": a crisis the winners can't fix |
| 0:31-0:44 | 45-55 | the rule used in reverse: the loophole wins |
| 0:44-0:52 | 15-25 | callback joke or chilling twist, hard cut on the last word |

**B. 90-110 s multi-twist (340-410 words)** (fits today's 90-120 s decision)
0:00-0:03 rule + action (12-15 w) | 0:03-0:10 cost / divide (25-30) | 0:10-0:26 3 escalating gags incl. a funny
grandma/neighbour/mayor (55-65) | 0:26-0:36 "And me?" + humiliation (35-40) | 0:36-0:48 "Until one day" + the strong
character fails (40-50) | 0:48-1:18 escalation, trap or training, twist 1 (110-125) | 1:18-1:32 rule in reverse (50-60) |
1:32-1:40 callback or cliffhanger, hard cut (20-30).

**First sentences (twist word by 2.5 s):**
1. "In this town, every [action] makes you [visible change]."
2. "In my world, [a number] floats above your head - and mine says [shocking value]."
3. "I [tiny normal act] and [absurd number] [people] [extreme consequence]."
4. "In this town, you don't [normal verb] - you [weird verb]."
5. "On your [age]th birthday everyone gets [cool thing]. Except me."
6. "Every time my [family member] [habit], the whole [town / day] [breaks]."
7. "In my world, rich kids get [absurd amount]. My family got [tiny number]."
8. "If you [break a simple rule] here, you instantly [absurd punishment]."
9. "My [family member] [weird condition], and honestly [that version] is better than the normal one."
10. "In this school, whoever [weird metric] runs the building - and I'm dead last."

**Endings (no outro needed):** home-door callback (the rule hits again at home); the grandma one-liner; the villain stuck
in his own punishment; the absurd reward in a normal room; the accidental repeat on the last frame; the bigger threat
using the same rule; a screen flash ("LOCATION FOUND"); a whispered warning; the unanswered question; the moral flip
(refuse the elite reward to stay with the people below).

## 4. Honest comment and share triggers

- Comments: an unresolved mystery ("What was behind the mountains?"), a background easter egg (a sign, a pet called
  Kevin), a loophole people will debate, or an open question baked into the action for at most 1.5 s ("Where should Dad
  sleepwalk him next?").
- Shares: absurdity in the first 3 s and a 3-second laugh or twist at the end.
- Gemini's view: drop the "Like, share, follow" outro and the "Comment X or Y" card - they cause end swipes and add no
  retention. **[decision: the like/share/follow ending and the two-option comment question are the owner's rule today]**
- Never: fake comments, misleading titles or first frames, buying engagement.

## 5. Packaging

- **YouTube title:** 2-6 words, Title Case, at most 1 emoji, no "PART 2", no hashtags ("Lying Makes You Shorter 📏",
  "Throw Food 🍌"). Description: one teaser line + #roblox #robloxstory #shorts.
- **Instagram caption:** a hook question or one punchy line + 4-5 hashtags (#roblox #robloxanimation #robloxstory
  #animatedstories #reels). Cover: the brightest frame with a clear shocked/funny face or a giant prop; never a dark
  interior or a text-heavy UI.
- **Frame 0:** sharp, bright, motion or an unmistakable rule prop (giant object, floating number, flying character).
- Answer YouTube's made-for-kids question truthfully (SKILL.md section 11).

## 6. Posting plan

- 3-4 uploads a week instead of 1 slow one: **2 full** (45-60 or 90 s, ~5 h each) + **2 lite** **[decision]**.
- **Lite format:** 35-45 s, 140-170 words, at most 2 locations and 3 characters, 1 rule + 2 gags + 1 twist, 25-30 shots
  (EP4 had 69) -> render ~45-60 min instead of 2.5 h **[Gemini estimate]**. Every lite video still needs its own story,
  sets dressed differently and its own hook (YouTube does not monetize template sameness).
- **Series:** never plan a Part 2 or put "PART 2" in a title. Make a sequel only if Part 1 breaks out and comments ask,
  and give it its own hook title ("The Giant Behind The Mountains").
- **Test:** a 4-video sprint of 45-55 s In-this-world Shorts made with the style guide. Check YouTube Studio at 48 h:
  first stayed-to-watch, then % viewed. One variable per experiment (growth loop).

## 7. Ten mistakes that keep small Roblox story channels stuck

1. A 5-second like/share/follow outro at the end. 2. A backstory first sentence instead of a rule or action.
3. A static frame 0 (phone screenshot, hallway, legs). 4. One flat TTS request for the whole script.
5. 17 meme pauses (~8 s of dead air), pace under 3.5 words/s. 6. Preachy moral lessons instead of absurd or twisty premises.
7. Characters standing and breathing (50+ idle-only beats). 8. Straight-on cameras and wide all-character framing.
9. 90-100 s runtimes before retention is earned. 10. "PART 2", ALL CAPS and hashtags in the title.
(1-10 were all true of our EP3/EP4; kit v5 fixes 3-5, 7 and 8 by default; 1 and 9 are owner decisions.)

## 8. Pre-publish checklist (pass / fail)

- [ ] frame 0: motion or the weird rule visible, no text wall
- [ ] rule or twist word spoken by 2.5 s; first word by 0.05 s
- [ ] 3.8-4.2 words/s, at most 6-8 meme pauses of <= 0.25-0.35 s
- [ ] runtime 45-60 s for sprints, 90-110 s only with 3+ escalations **[decision]**
- [ ] premise >= 20/25
- [ ] outro: owner's current rule applied **[decision]**
- [ ] title: 2-6 words, Title Case, <= 1 emoji, no PART 2, no hashtags
- [ ] <= 15 % idle-only shots, <= 20 % straight-on shots, 0 floating props (`tools/ep_viral_check.py` + contact sheet)
- [ ] the ending makes you want to replay it or send it
