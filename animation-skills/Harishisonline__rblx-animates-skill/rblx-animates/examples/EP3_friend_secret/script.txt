# EP3 I Told ONE Secret. By Lunch, Everyone Knew. (Blox City Day 02). Approved 2026-11-16. Captions text.
I told my best friend one secret. By lunch, the whole school knew. And he got paid for it.
Max and I had been best friends since kindergarten. Same lunch table. Same bus seat. A secret handshake with eleven steps.
So one night, on a call, I told him the thing nobody knew. I'm fourteen, and I can't sleep without my night-light. It's shaped like a duck. "Your secret's safe with me," Max said.
At Blox City High, there's an account called Blox Tea. Chase runs it. Chase is rich. And Chase pays five hundred coins for every secret you send him.
Next morning, my phone would not stop buzzing. Blox Tea had posted a drawing of me, hugging a duck. Two hundred comments. At lunch, the whole cafeteria quacked when I walked in. Everyone laughed. Max didn't look up.
Then I saw his head. A brand new limited hat. Five hundred coins. I sat down across from him. "Was I worth five hundred coins?" He stared at his tray. "It was a limited, bro."
I didn't talk to him for two weeks. Then came the class camping trip.
Night one, a storm knocked out every light at camp. Pitch black. Somebody screamed. Then everybody screamed.
I opened my bag and switched on the duck. One by one, the whole class sat down around it. Even Chase. Chase was shaking. Chase was holding my sleeve.
Someone held up a phone. "Post it, Leo. Blox Tea pays five hundred." I looked at Chase. I knew exactly how that felt. "Nah. Secrets aren't for sale."
The next morning, Max found me by the tents. He held out five hundred coins. "I'm sorry. Take it." I pushed it back. "Keep it. You'll need it to buy a new best friend."
That week, Chase deleted Blox Tea himself. And half my class ordered duck night-lights.
